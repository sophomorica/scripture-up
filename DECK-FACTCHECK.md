# Deck fact-check

Checked 26 Sep 2026 against the scripture text on churchofjesuschrist.org (the Church's published King James Bible and the standard Book of Mormon / Doctrine and Covenants text). Each row is one item from the "Accuracy flags: summary" in the design pack. The live Book of Mormon deck is the shipped 18 cards. None of those 18 was simply wrong, so every id, reference, and clue in that deck is unchanged.

## 1. `bom-3ne-11-10` — Book of Mormon (live)

- **Card said:** reference `3 Nephi 11:10`, clue "He speaks his own name". The design seed had written `3 Nephi 11:10–11`.
- **Verse:** [3 Nephi 11](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/3-ne/11?lang=eng). Verse 9: "And it came to pass that he stretched forth his hand and spake unto the people, saying:" Verse 10: "Behold, I am Jesus Christ, whom the prophets testified shall come into the world." Verse 11 continues "I am the light and the life of the world" and does not say the name.
- **Verdict:** The shipped card is right. The name is spoken in verse 10. The seed's range 10–11 adds a verse the clue does not need.
- **Fix:** None. Id, reference, and clue stay.

## 2. `nt-john-11-35` — New Testament (coming soon)

- **Card said:** reference `John 11:35`, clue "The two-word verse".
- **Verse:** [John 11:35](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/john/11?lang=eng): "Jesus wept." That is two words in the King James text the Church site publishes.
- **Verdict:** True of that King James verse, and not true of every English translation. The flag asked for a translation-neutral clue or an explicit King James label.
- **Fix:** Clue is now "Two words in the King James". Reference unchanged.

## 3. `sty-jonah` — Stories (proposed)

- **Card said:** answer "Jonah and the Whale", clue "Swallowed, three days, spat out".
- **Verse:** [Jonah 1:17](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jonah/1?lang=eng): "Now the Lord had prepared a great fish to swallow up Jonah. And Jonah was in the belly of the fish three days and three nights." [Jonah 2:10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/ot/jonah/2?lang=eng): "And the Lord spake unto the fish, and it vomited out Jonah upon the dry land." [Matthew 12:40](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/matt/12?lang=eng) (King James) says "the whale's belly".
- **Verdict:** The story the card is about says "great fish". "Whale" is the King James word in Matthew 12:40, not the word in Jonah. "Spat out" matches Jonah 2:10. The three days match Jonah 1:17.
- **Fix:** Answer is now "Jonah and the Great Fish". The clue already matches the fish verses, so it stays.

## 4. `hro-mormon` — Heroes (proposed)

- **Card said:** answer "Mormon", clue "Led armies as a teen; abridged it all".
- **Verse:** [Mormon 2:2](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/morm/2?lang=eng): "in my sixteenth year I did go forth at the head of an army of the Nephites". Mormon 2:1 says he was "young". The sixteenth year is about age 15.
- **Verdict:** "As a teen" is accurate and does not invent an exact age.
- **Fix:** None.

## 5. `plc-zarahemla` — Places (proposed)

- **Card said:** answer "Zarahemla", clue "Major Nephite city and land".
- **Verse:** [Omni 1](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/omni/1?lang=eng) introduces the people of Zarahemla and the land. [Alma 53:10](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/53?lang=eng): "brought down into the land of Zarahemla". [Helaman 1:27](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/hel/1?lang=eng): "they had come into the center of the land, and had taken the capital city which was the city of Zarahemla".
- **Verdict:** It is both a city and a land. The clue does not say "capital". Helaman 1:27 does use "capital city", so that word would not have been a falsehood, but the clue already follows the flag and needs no change.
- **Fix:** None.

## 6. `sty-stripling` — Stories (proposed)

- **Card said:** answer "The Stripling Warriors", clue "2,000 young soldiers, faithful mothers".
- **Verse:** [Alma 53:18](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/53?lang=eng): "there were two thousand of those young men". Alma 53:22: "his two thousand stripling soldiers". [Alma 56:5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/56?lang=eng): "two thousand of these young men". [Alma 57:6](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/alma/57?lang=eng): "sixty of the sons of the Ammonites who had come to join their brethren, my little band of two thousand" (2,060 after the later joiners).
- **Verdict:** 2,000 is the number when they enlisted and marched. 2,060 is a later total. The clue is right for the first number.
- **Fix:** None.

## 7. `par-olive-tree` — Parables (proposed)

- **Card said:** answer "The Allegory of the Olive Tree", clue "Jacob 5: grafting and pruning".
- **Verse:** [Jacob 5](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/jacob/5?lang=eng) heading: "Jacob quotes Zenos relative to the allegory of the tame and wild olive trees". The chapter is grafting, pruning, and the vineyard. It is an allegory in the Book of Mormon, not a Gospel parable.
- **Verdict:** The answer already calls it an allegory, and the clue names Jacob 5. Dropping it would be a deck-scope choice, not a correction of a false card.
- **Fix:** None. It stays in the proposed Parables deck, labelled as an allegory.

## 8. `dc-89` — Doctrine and Covenants (coming soon)

- **Card said:** reference `D&C 89` (no verse), clue "Health code: run, not weary".
- **Verse:** [Doctrine and Covenants 89](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/89?lang=eng) is the whole section "known as the Word of Wisdom". Verse 18 promises "health". Verse 20: "And shall run and not be weary, and shall walk and not faint."
- **Verdict:** A section reference with no verse is what the card claims, and the clue matches verses 18 and 20. The forehead line is the string `D&C 89`. The card face draws that string in a FittedBox, so a missing verse number does not need a special case.
- **Fix:** None in the card text. The short form "D&C" stays on the card because it fits the forehead; the deck title stays "Doctrine and Covenants".

## 9. Study-list overlap — Doctrine and Covenants and Hard Mode

- **Card said:** Several public references in those decks also appear on official study lists (for example D&C 4:2, D&C 58:27, 2 Nephi 31:20, Mosiah 3:19). Clues are original short phrases.
- **Verse:** Not a single verse. The references themselves are public scripture.
- **Verdict:** Using a public reference is fine. Grouping, labelling, or ordering a deck as an official study program would not be. Nothing in the deck files or the UI uses a program name.
- **Fix:** None on the cards. A lint test rejects program names in `lib/` and `assets/decks/`, and rejects a clue that contains its own answer word or its own reference.
