### Promotional text (170)

### Description (4000)

Scripture Up turns scripture practice into a party game for families, classes, and youth nights.

One player holds the phone sideways on their forehead with the screen facing the team. The team reads the phrase on the card out loud and may give any hints, but never says the book, chapter, or verse. The guesser names the reference. The team checks it against the answer on the card and decides if it is right.

Tilt the screen down for a correct guess. Tilt it up to pass. Bring the phone back to level and the next card is live. Prefer buttons? Turn on Tap buttons instead of tilt.

FOUR BOOKS, 100 PASSAGES
Play the Book of Mormon, Old Testament, New Testament, or Doctrine and Covenants on its own, or pick ALL to deal all 100 passages without a repeat. They are the same 100 passages Seminary Sidekick teaches.

QUICK ROUNDS
Choose 30, 60, or 90 seconds. The clock warns you in the last ten seconds with color, a pulse, and a tick in your hand. Then see your score, tap any passed card for its phrase, and chase your personal best for each book and round length.

RECORD THE TEAM (OPTIONAL)
Recording is off until you turn it on. Turn it on to film the team giving clues, then save or delete the clip after the round. Clips stay on your phone. The app never uploads them.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no in-app purchases, and no tracking. Your scores and clips stay on your device.

Made by Narrow Road Studios. Not affiliated with The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints.

## App Privacy (nutrition label)

Answer in App Store Connect, App Privacy:

- **Do you or your third-party partners collect data from this app?** No.
- Result: **Data Not Collected.**

Why that is accurate (read from the 1.2.0 code):

- Dependencies: `audioplayers`, `camera`, `path_provider`, `sensors_plus`, `video_player`, `url_launcher`. No analytics, crash reporting, ads, or account SDKs.
- The app makes no network requests. The only link out is the About screen's Seminary Sidekick button, which opens seminarysidekick.com in Safari. The UTM tags on that URL are read by our website, not by the app.
- Camera and microphone are used only when "Record the team" is on. Clips are written to the app's documents folder (`recordings/`) and never leave the phone. Apple counts data as "collected" only when it is transmitted off the device, so on-device recordings are not collected.
- Scores and personal bests are a local file.
- Tracking: No.

Usage strings already in Info.plist:
- Camera: "Scripture Up records the people giving clues during a round. The clip stays on this phone."
- Microphone: "Scripture Up records room audio with the round video. The clip stays on this phone."

Export compliance: `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false` is set, so no question at upload.
